# Changelog

# 📦 0.10.0

## 📰 General changes and notes

**BACKUP BACKUP BACKUP**

0.10.0 is a large update pass: 204 mods updated, 24 added and 7 removed, on NeoForge 21.1.244. Make sure you have a backup of your world before doing this update. There's no returning to a previous version after updating if you do not have a BACKUP.

> [!WARNING]
> **Eternal Starlight worlds do not carry over.** 0.9.4 shipped Eternal Starlight 0.6.1, whose Starlight dimension used the `es_gen` generator and `es_biomes` biome source. 0.8.1 replaces both (`biome_based` / `multi_noise`). An existing world stores the old ids, which no longer exist, so its Starlight dimension can no longer load and a new one is generated.

> **T.O Magic 'n Extras (Traveloptics) has been removed.** Its only 1.21.1 build dates from March 2025, before the Iron's Spells 3.16 update this release ships. Removing it takes 188 items, 65 spells, 26 mobs and 50 effects with it: its weapons, curios and materials disappear from inventories, ender chests and storage, its spells are stripped from spell books, and its bosses no longer spawn.

> [!NOTE]
> **Mysticrift: Pharaoh's Legacy has been removed.** It injected its Sahara Desert biome into the overworld, so removing it outright would stop existing worlds loading. The pack now ships a replacement biome under the same id, keeping the terrain, colours and ores; the mod's own mobs, trees and music are gone.

Relics updated to 0.12, which reworked how relic abilities are stored. Its quest chapter and item data were rebuilt to match, and Reliquified Twilight Forest was removed because it has no compatible build.
